Six-month pilot: 120 people completed introductory training; 18 completed advanced maintenance training (SRC-001). Intentionally non-random follow-up survey of 80 respondents: 62 reported education use, 41 income-related use, 55 service use; respondents could report multiple uses (SRC-002). Pilot had no comparison group; self-reported uses cannot establish training caused outcomes. Preliminary findings—not a causal estimate.
