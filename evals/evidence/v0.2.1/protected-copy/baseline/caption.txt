Six-month pilot: 120 people completed introductory training; 18 completed advanced maintenance training (SRC-001). Intentionally non-random follow-up survey: 80 respondents self-reported education (62), income-related (41), and service (55) use; respondents could report multiple uses (SRC-002). No comparison group; self-reported uses cannot establish that training caused outcomes. Preliminary findings—not a causal estimate.
