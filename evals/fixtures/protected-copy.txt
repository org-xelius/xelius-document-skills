In a six-month pilot, 120 people completed introductory training and 18 completed advanced maintenance training (SRC-001). An intentionally non-random follow-up survey of 80 respondents recorded 62 reporting education use, 41 income-related use, and 55 service use (SRC-002); respondents could report more than one use. The pilot had no comparison group, and these self-reported uses cannot establish that the training caused the outcomes. Preliminary findings—not a causal estimate.
