# Message-led Headlines

A headline should state what the audience should understand, not label the topic.

| Topic label | Message-led headline |
| --- | --- |
| Connectivity findings | Unreliable connectivity remains the first barrier to digital work |
| Survey results | Device affordability excludes the same groups most ready to participate |
| Our approach | Local training made the network operable without permanent outside support |
| Recommendations | Three coordinated investments can convert access into income |

## Test

- Can a reader understand the argument by reading headlines only?
- Is the claim specific enough to be proved?
- Would a presenter plausibly say it aloud?
- Does it avoid jargon, slogans, and formulaic repetition?
- Does the slide's evidence directly support it?

If a headline needs "and" to hold two claims, split the slide.
