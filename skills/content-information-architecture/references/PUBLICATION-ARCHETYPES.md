# Publication Archetypes

| Archetype | Communication job | Typical structure | Evidence emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Annual report | Account for a period and build confidence | review, leadership, strategy chapters, impact, community, governance, financials, outlook | metrics, stories, maps, audited figures |
| Research report | Explain methods, findings, and implications | front matter, executive summary, context, methods, findings, conclusions, references, annexes | traceability, charts, tables, limitations |
| Case study | Explain what happened, how, and what others can learn | summary, context, intervention, partnership, implementation, outcomes, learning, next steps | authentic images, concrete measures, voices |
| Policy brief | Enable a decision | issue, evidence, implications, options, recommendations, methods and sources | concise claims, comparison, action |
| White paper | Frame an issue and develop an argument | thesis, context, thematic analysis, implications, recommendations, endnotes | literature, examples, policy reasoning |
| Toolkit | Enable repeatable action | orientation, modules, steps, tools, templates, examples, checklist | instructions and reusable resources |
| Training manual | Support facilitation and learning | outcomes, modules, activities, timing, materials, assessment, references | procedures and learning checks |

Select one primary archetype. Borrowing a component does not change the archetype.
