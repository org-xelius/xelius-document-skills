# Content Planning

Inventory sources using `assets/schemas/source-inventory.schema.json`; capture claims, data, quotes, methods, caveats, images, rights, duplicates, superseded files, and unreadable sources. Assign stable evidence IDs and locators.

For reports, read [PUBLICATION-ARCHETYPES.md](PUBLICATION-ARCHETYPES.md) and produce `publication-plan.json`: hierarchy, section purposes, page families, evidence forms, annex, and references.

For decks, read [PRESENTATION-ARCHETYPES.md](PRESENTATION-ARCHETYPES.md), [STORYLINE-DESIGN.md](STORYLINE-DESIGN.md), and [MESSAGE-LED-HEADLINES.md](MESSAGE-LED-HEADLINES.md). Produce `deck-storyline.json` with one purpose, message, dominant evidence form, and transition per slide. Keep report and deck plans distinct.

Validate inventories, evidence registers, publication plans, and storylines against relevant schemas in `assets/schemas/`. Show contradictions, missing evidence, and realistic length at the structure checkpoint. Move detail to annex/notes only if the main narrative stays honest. Critical gaps yield provisional plans with blockers, not fabricated content.
