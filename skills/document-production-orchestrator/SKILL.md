# Document Production Orchestrator

Coordinate only the stages the requested deliverable needs.

Select **create**, **revise**, **review**, or **export**. Create establishes evidence, structure, design, representative output, production, and QA. Revise reuses valid plans and preserves approved content/identity. Review returns findings without edits. Export routes directly to PDF production or presentation build/export.

Read [ROUTING.md](references/ROUTING.md). Narrow caption, reference, table, or deck repairs do not need the full pipeline. Check installed specialists; use available equivalent capabilities and state gaps rather than assuming the collection is present.

Record audience, purpose/action, outputs/length, brand mode, protected content, requirements, and checkpoint preference in `working/project-manifest.json`. Ask only for consequential missing decisions; uninterrupted work records decisions internally without weakening QA.

1. Inspect sources/assets; share one inventory and evidence register across formats. Plan only unresolved evidence/structure.
2. Give reports and decks separate plans/composition; never turn report pages into slides.
3. Preserve or establish the design contract; test representative dense, sparse, data-led, and image-led content before scaling.
4. Route copy/citations to content architecture, charts/tables to data visualisation, conceptual vectors to SVG, authentic raster evidence to imagery, and deck building to professional presentation.
5. Render and inspect every surface, repair at source, and re-render. Validate canonical QA before deriving status.
6. Deliver editable sources, exports, previews, `qa/qa-report.json`, and a production report naming validation, dependencies, and limitations.

Read [TOOL-DETECTION-AND-FALLBACKS.md](references/TOOL-DETECTION-AND-FALLBACKS.md) when selecting tools; `scripts/detect-environment.mjs --json` reports capabilities.

Preserve caveats, rights, credits, citations, and uncertainty. Never silently substitute synthetic documentary imagery. Missing checks require honest limitations, not false passes. Pause for missing authority or consequential unresolved choices, not routine implementation.
