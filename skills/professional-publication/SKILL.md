# Professional Publication

Compose a reading experience, not decorated pages. Use **create**, scoped **revise**, or findings-only **review**. Preserve approved content/design in revisions; route export-only work to PDF production.

Read [REPORT-COMPOSITION.md](references/REPORT-COMPOSITION.md). Reuse valid evidence and plans. Establish audience, size, print/screen use, accessibility needs, and an output contract: type minima, placed figures, contrast, margins, language, and PDF properties.

For new systems/major redesigns, agree architecture/direction and test a representative spread before scaling. Stress-test the longest heading, densest table, longest label, complex figure, and sparse page. Respect checkpoint preferences.

Default to editable semantic HTML/CSS and SVG; use `templates/report-starter/` when useful. Fixed-layout PPTX needs justification. Retain source lists, assets, and build instructions with PDF/previews.

Keep figures with captions and tables with titles, repeated headers, notes, and sources. Prevent orphaned headings; avoid split rows/widows where practical. Never shrink body text below the approved minimum or stretch thin content to meet a page target. Balance density/whitespace; repeat layouts when the reader task benefits.

Preserve facts, caveats, citations, and data. Keep evidence IDs, implementation labels, and QA disclaimers outside reader-facing copy unless requested. Use content architecture for copy/references and data visualisation for charts/tables.

Export through PDF production. Inspect every page at full size and in sequence: pagination, hierarchy, typography, figures, references, links, bookmarks, furniture, print safety, and pacing. Repair at source and re-render. Missing tools/checks mean limitations; export success alone is not completed QA.
