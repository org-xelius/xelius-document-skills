# Design Direction Profiles

Use these contracts to begin a direction discussion. Do not treat them as fixed themes. Read `../assets/design-direction-profiles.json` from this reference directory, or `assets/design-direction-profiles.json` from the skill directory, for the machine-readable form.

## Selection

1. Identify the output, audience, viewing distance, evidence density, emotional temperature, and brand mode.
2. Select one primary profile and, only when justified, one secondary influence.
3. Translate the profile into output-specific tokens.
4. Record what is protected, flexible, and prohibited.
5. Test the direction on a representative spread or slide sequence.

## Reference-publication lessons

The supplied corpus demonstrates five useful but non-interchangeable systems:

- A short community-connectivity case study succeeds through authentic photography, generous narrative pages, a stable footer, and restrained colour.
- A Rwanda connectivity research report uses high-density text, repeated chart grammar, bright evidence highlights, extensive front matter, and annex tables.
- An Internet Society annual report uses chapter colour families, modular impact blocks, maps, photography, partner stories, and a distinct financial section.
- The Global Index on Responsible AI uses atmospheric section transitions, large numbered findings, a repeated analytical grid, insight callouts, and dense annex heatmaps.
- A policy white paper on AI and Black Americans uses a restrained two-column editorial system, margin callouts, section-colour accents, and endnotes.

Do not copy logos, layouts, illustrations, or proprietary brand assets. Abstract only the production lesson: match density, pacing, evidence form, and audience need.

## Contracts

### Human-centred development

Lead with agency, not beneficiary tropes. Use documentary images as evidence. Pair open pages with concise impact measures. Preserve credits and contextual captions.

### Institutional research

Prioritize auditability. Stabilize running furniture, chart language, table hierarchy, source notes, and annex navigation. Variation should occur at section boundaries, not at the cost of comparison.

### Editorial technology

Use one distinctive atmosphere to connect abstract subject matter. Keep long-form pages sober. Avoid glowing brains, robots, pseudo-neural decoration, and gradients unrelated to the content.

### Premium annual report

Build a chapter system that can hold people, programs, maps, metrics, partners, governance, and financials without making them look identical. Let the brand stretch without fragmenting.

### Evidence-led policy brief

Make the decision path visible: issue, evidence, implication, option, recommendation. Provide quotation-ready claims and compact sources. Keep supporting detail accessible.

### Modern executive presentation

Design for a room. Make the title state the message, give each slide one job, reduce simultaneous variables, and close with an explicit decision or action.
