# Colour and Contrast

Define semantic roles before hex values: canvas, surface, text, muted text, rule, primary, accent, positive, caution, negative, data-neutral, and categorical series.

## Rules

- Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for digital text whenever accessibility is in scope; use 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text and meaningful graphical objects.
- Do not encode data by colour alone. Pair with direct labels, shape, pattern, or position.
- Test colours on projectors, office printers, grayscale output, and over photography when those outputs matter.
- Keep functional colours stable across report and deck even when their visual intensity differs.
- Record CMYK or spot-colour expectations when print production requires them.

## Anti-defaults

Reject purple-blue AI gradients, neon glows, glass effects, and rainbow charts unless the content or established brand supports them. Prefer a small, intentional palette with a documented emphasis budget.
