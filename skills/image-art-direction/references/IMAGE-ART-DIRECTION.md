# Image Art Direction

## Inventory fields

Asset ID, file, pixel dimensions, aspect ratio, colour mode, capture date, photographer, credit, rights, caption, people or location sensitivity, duplicate group, focal point, protected region, approved uses, and replacement status.

## Resolution

Judge resolution at placed size. Use roughly 150 ppi as a screen or presentation warning threshold and 250–300 ppi for high-quality print, subject to production needs. Do not treat interpolation as new detail.

## Crop protocol

1. Define destination ratio and safe area.
2. Identify faces, hands, devices, text, landmarks, and contextual relationships.
3. Place focal interest away from overlaid copy.
4. Preserve gaze and movement space.
5. Check every crop at final size.

## Authenticity

Documentary images must depict the actual people, place, event, or program claimed. Synthetic or stock images may be used only as clearly non-documentary illustration with appropriate disclosure and rights.
